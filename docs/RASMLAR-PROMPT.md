# Demo rasmlar uchun promptlar (ChatGPT / DALL·E / Midjourney)

Bu promptlar demo aksiyalar uchun bir xil uslubdagi rasmlar yaratish uchun. Haqiqiy bizneslar esa o‘z rasmlarini kabinetdan yuklaydi.

## Qanday ishlatiladi

1. ChatGPT’ga avval quyidagi **umumiy uslub** matnini yuboring, keyin rasmlarni bittadan so‘rang (bir xabarda 1 ta rasm eng sifatli chiqadi).
2. Har bir rasmni ko‘rsatilgan **fayl nomi** bilan saqlang (JPG yoki WebP, eni kamida 1600 px, 4:3 nisbat).
3. Rasmlarni chatga yuboring — men ularni `public/demo/` papkasiga joylab, demo aksiyalarga ulayman.

## Umumiy uslub (har promptdan oldin yoki bir marta boshida)

> I need a series of photos for a food and lifestyle deals app in Uzbekistan. Style for every image: realistic professional photography, natural soft daylight, warm color palette with coral-orange and cream accents, shallow depth of field, clean uncluttered background, appetizing and premium look, 4:3 landscape, no text, no logos, no watermarks, no people's faces.
